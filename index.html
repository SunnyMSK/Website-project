01  About

Who we are

The OCA Student Council is a special club for campus life — planning events, running community service projects, and bringing student feedback directly to faculty and administration.

The Council is fairly new, founded in September 2026, and it is still in its early stages. Most arrangements are temporary for now, put in place to keep the Council running.
